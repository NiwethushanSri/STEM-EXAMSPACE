# Design System: Stem Examspace

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Open Exam Hall"**

A bright, clean education site in the category-standard manner of Save My Exams, rebuilt in Stem Examspace's own colours: logo navy for words, logo blue for every action, and the emblem's green, teal and orange as soft tints and as the performance code in charts. Sections alternate white and soft grey; everything in the interface is one friendly sans at confident weights; buttons, chips and tabs are fully rounded pills; cards are gently rounded, hairline-bordered and lifted by a faint navy-tinted shadow. The warmth comes from hand-drawn navy line drawings above the steps and a single blue hand-drawn ring around one word of each section heading, both of which draw themselves as they scroll in. The copy is calm and adult: plain statements of what happens, no exclamation, no hype.

Proof is shown, not claimed: a working student analysis dashboard, the live timetable with seats and prices, an interactive marking screen on a pictured tablet, the result as it arrives on a pictured phone, and a pictured index card. The pictured objects (script, tablet and docked pen, phone, store badges, index card) keep their own realistic materials, kept apart from the interface palette under the `artefact-` prefix; the phone screen even uses the phone's own system font, because it pictures a phone. Every example is labelled as example data.

This system currently covers the public home page (`src/app/page.tsx`, `src/components/home/`), scoped under the `.home` class. The older staff and account pages are not on this system yet; do not read their styles as part of it, and do not restyle them from this file without a decision to do so. A premium navy-and-serif system was tried and retired; nothing from it is part of this file.

**Key Characteristics:**
- White and soft-grey bands, faint blue wash behind the hero only.
- One interface family, Plus Jakarta Sans: 800 headings, 18px body.
- Navy (#10224f family) for headings, blue for every action; navy fills for selected chips and subject tabs.
- Pill buttons, chips, tabs and status labels; 16 to 18px cards, 22px for the analysis dashboard.
- Emblem tints by subject: maths green, sciences teal, computing orange; the same hues grade performance (strong green, mid orange, weak red).
- In every chart: marks are solid blue, the cohort is a dotted slate line, the forecast is dashed orange.
- Hand-drawn blue ring on one heading word; navy line drawings with people.
- Realistic pictured artefacts: tablet with docked pen, flagship phone, red examiner pen and Kalam handwriting.

## Colors

A white, airy palette with one blue doing all the clicking, navy doing all the talking, and three emblem hues coding subject, seat status and performance.

### Primary
- **Logo Blue** (primary): every button, link, selected marking tab, switch-on, step number, tick circle, focus ring, the hand-drawn heading ring, and the marks line and points in charts. Hover deepens to **Pressed Blue** (primary-hover), which also titles the focus box; **Blue Mist** (primary-tint) is the hover/selected wash, the step-card divider and the focus-box fill; **Hero Wash** (hero-wash) fades the hero top from blue to white.

### Secondary
- **Emblem Green** (green / green-tint / green-deep): maths subject tone, seats-open pill, live badge dot, full-marks bar, strong-topic bar, rising KPI delta, text highlight.
- **Emblem Teal** (teal / teal-tint / teal-deep): science subject tone and the score ring on the phone result.

### Tertiary
- **Emblem Orange** (orange / orange-tint / orange-deep / orange-ink): computing subject tone, few-seats pill, waiting badge dot, part-marks and mid-topic bars, the forecast line and forecast figure, the marker's-note box (orange-ink body text on orange-tint).
- **Booked Red** (red-tint / red-deep): fully booked and closed pills, falling KPI delta, weak-topic bar.

### Neutral
- **Logo Navy** (ink): headings, nav, titles, selected filter chip and subject tab, figures, chart value labels.
- **Slate Text** (text): body copy. **Muted Slate** (muted): ledes, sub-lines, captions, chart axes, the cohort line, footer links.
- **White** (white) for cards, dashboard and header; **Soft Grey** (bg-soft) for alternate bands, segment track, KPI tiles, topic-bar tracks and nav hover.
- **Hairline** (border) for every card, chip and chart gridline; **Dashed Line** (border-dashed) for the empty-timetable box; **Switch Off** (switch-off) and **Bar Track** (bar-track) for controls.

### Artefact-local (pictured objects only)
Examiner pen red, student pen blue, printed paper inks, script rules, marking-panel lines, tablet screen grey, ID-photo grey and silhouette, the active-answer highlight yellow; the near-black device glass, bezel and home indicator, the tablet's aluminium edge and camera, the phone's titanium band, side buttons and camera island; the white docked pen's body, band, edge, cone and nib. The coming-soon store badges take the device black because they picture store badges. None of these appear on interface chrome.

### Named Rules
**The One Blue Rule.** If it can be clicked, it is Logo Blue; selected filter chips and selected subject tabs fill navy. Nothing decorative competes for that blue except the heading ring and the marks line in charts.

**The Emblem Code Rule.** Green, teal and orange mean subject (maths, sciences, computing) or seat status, always as tint background with deep-tone foreground. In charts and bars the solids grade performance: green strong (70% and above), orange middling (55 to 69%), red-deep weak (below 55%). Never as large fills.

**The Forecast Is Orange Rule.** Anything forecast is orange: a dashed orange line ending in a hollow orange point, an orange-deep figure. Actual marks are solid blue with filled points; the cohort is a dotted muted line.

**The Pen Stays On Paper Rule.** Examiner red (artefact-pen-red) appears only on pictured scripts and drawings, never on interface chrome; status red is red-deep on red-tint.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Plus Jakarta Sans (with Segoe UI, system-ui, sans-serif)
**Body Font:** Plus Jakarta Sans
**Handwriting (pictured script only):** Kalam (with Segoe Print, cursive)
**Phone screen (pictured phone only):** the phone's system stack, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, SF Pro Text, Helvetica Neue, Segoe UI, system-ui

**Character:** One rounded, friendly geometric sans carries everything; hierarchy comes from weight (800 against 400/600) and navy against slate, not from a second family.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (800, clamp 36 to 52px, 1.1, -0.025em): the hero headline only.
- **Headline** (800, clamp 30 to 40px, 1.15, -0.02em): one per section, balanced wrap, one ringed word.
- **Figure** (800, 30px, tabular): the marking score and the dashboard KPI figures; **figure-sm** (24px) for KPIs under 760px.
- **Title-lg** (800, 22px): step, audience-card and empty-state titles, prices.
- **Title** (800, 19px): paper and results titles.
- **Lead** (400, clamp 18 to 20px, max 50ch): hero paragraph.
- **Body** (400, 18px, 1.55; 17px under 760px, max 62 to 70ch): copy and ledes; body-sm (17px) for step, list and checklist text, and dashboard card titles at 700.
- **Button** (700, 17px; 16px compact in header and cards). **Nav** (600, 16px).
- **Label** (600 to 700, 15px): chips, subject tabs, segments, facts, topic names, focus text, card sub-lines, footer.
- **Caption** (14px): figure captions, badge, KPI labels, chart legend and hints, dashboard notes, footer note. **Pill** (700, 13px): seat status, counts, subject-tab percentages.
- **Chart value** (700, 12px) and **chart axis** (400, 11px): SVG labels inside the analysis chart only, in navy and muted slate.
- **Artefact sizes** (pictured objects only): script question 14.5px, meta 12.5px, card fine 11.5px, card brand 12px uppercase 0.14em, card index 21px, Kalam answer 20px, Kalam margin mark 24px bold; store badge 19px/700 with a 12px/500 "Coming soon to" line; phone large title 30px/700, status bar 15px/600, nav 16px, body 14px, footnote 13px, tab labels 10.5px/600, all in the phone's system stack.

### Named Rules
**The One Voice Rule.** Plus Jakarta Sans is the only interface face. Kalam is handwriting pictured on a script, and the phone system stack is the type of a pictured phone screen; neither ever sets an interface label, heading or button.

**The Numbers Line Up Rule.** Prices, marks, scores, percentages, KPIs, counts, chart labels and index numbers use tabular numerals.

## Layout

Centered container of 1180px (header 1280px) with a fluid gutter. Sections breathe at clamp(64px, 8vw, 104px) and alternate white and soft grey. The hero is a 1.3 : 0.8 split (copy left, three role cards right). The analysis dashboard sits full container width under a centered heading: subject tabs and a muted "Example student" line on top, four KPI tiles in a row (two by two under 760px), then a 1.35 : 1 split of progress chart and topic breakdown (stacked at 1024px). Marking, results-on-the-phone and index-card sections are asymmetric two-column splits that stack at 1024px. Papers sit in a three-column grid (two at 1024px, one at 760px); the audience cards sit three across (one column at 900px); the three steps sit side by side with drawings overhanging their tops, becoming horizontal drawing-plus-text rows at 1024px and stacked at 560px. Under 860px the header nav hides and a horizontally scrolling pill section menu appears below the header. Sticky header 72px (64px on phones); sections carry scroll-margin to clear it. Card grids use a 20px gap; tile rows inside the dashboard use 12px.

## Elevation & Depth

Mostly flat with one soft lift. Cards and the dashboard rest on a two-layer navy-tinted ambient shadow; tiles inside them (KPI tiles, dashboard cards) are flat, separated by soft grey or a hairline. Hover adds a blue border and a 2px rise, never a heavier shadow. Pictured devices take deeper, longer drop shadows because they are things lying on the page, and draw their metal edges as zero-blur rings.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Card** (`box-shadow: 0 1px 2px rgb(16 34 79 / 0.05), 0 8px 24px -6px rgb(16 34 79 / 0.1)`): role, step, paper, audience, FAQ cards and the analysis dashboard.
- **Segment thumb** (`box-shadow: 0 1px 3px rgb(16 34 79 / 0.16)`): the selected option in the level segment.
- **Tablet** (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 2px #3b3e46, 0 0 0 3.5px #1c1e23, 0 36px 60px -28px rgb(16 34 79 / 0.6)`), **Phone** (three titanium rings, then `0 50px 80px -32px rgb(16 34 79 / 0.55), 0 12px 24px -12px rgb(16 34 79 / 0.3)`), **Docked pen** (`filter: drop-shadow(0 3px 3px rgb(16 34 79 / 0.25))`) and **Index card** (`box-shadow: 0 24px 40px -18px rgb(16 34 79 / 0.35)`): pictured objects only.
- **Script sheet** (`0 1px 4px rgb(0 0 0 / 0.1)`) and **App card** (`0 1px 2px rgb(16 34 79 / 0.06)`): paper on the tablet screen, cards on the phone screen.

### Named Rules
**The Soft Lift Rule.** Interface depth is one ambient, blurred, navy-tinted shadow; hard or offset drop shadows do not belong here. Zero-blur spreads are allowed only as edges (device metal, the ghost button ring, the highlight's side extension), never as depth.

## Shapes

Everything interactive is a full pill (999px): buttons, chips, subject tabs, segments, marking tabs, nav links, section-menu links, status pills, switch track, and the topic and progress bars. Containers are gently rounded: 22px for the analysis dashboard, 18px for content cards and panels, 16px for role, FAQ and dashboard cards, 14px for KPI tiles and the index card, 12px for icon tiles, the focus box, notes and store badges, 8px for mark rows and the focus ring. Circles (50%) for step numbers, tick dots, FAQ plus icons, badge dots and chart points. Edges are 1px hairlines; the only dashed edge is the empty-timetable box. The pictured devices keep true proportions: the tablet at 30px (22px on phones), the phone with elliptical corners (16% / 7.8% body, 13% / 6.2% screen) and a pill camera island. The ringed heading word and the step drawings are freehand strokes (pathLength-normalised so they can draw in).

## Components

### Buttons
Friendly and solid.
- **Shape:** full pill (999px), min-height 48px.
- **Primary:** Logo Blue fill, white 17px/700 text, 12px 26px; compact 44px (header) and 42px (paper cards) at 16px.
- **Hover / Focus:** fill deepens to Pressed Blue over 160ms ease-out; 3px blue focus outline, 3px offset.
- **Ghost:** white fill, blue text, 2px inset blue ring; hover takes Blue Mist.
- **Text link:** bold blue, 2px underline at 4px offset, with a trailing arrow.

### Chips and subject tabs
- **Style:** white pill, hairline border, 15px slate or navy text; filter chips carry an optional 26px circular subject-tint icon and a count pill; subject tabs carry a muted 13px percentage.
- **State:** hover gives a blue border (filter chips also take Blue Mist); selected (aria-pressed or aria-selected) fills navy with white text and a translucent white count.

### Segmented control, tabs and switch
Soft-grey pill track with 4px inset. Level segment: selected option becomes a white thumb with navy text and the segment-thumb shadow. Marking tabs: white track, selected tab fills Logo Blue. Switch: 44 by 26px pill, off grey, on blue, white knob slides 18px; it also toggles the cohort line on the chart.

### Cards / Containers
- **Corner Style:** 18px (papers, steps, audience cards, results), 16px (role cards, FAQ).
- **Background:** white on either band.
- **Shadow Strategy:** the Card shadow (see Elevation).
- **Border:** 1px hairline; hover on linked cards turns it blue.
- **Internal Padding:** 22px papers, 28px audience cards, 18px 20px role cards.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** use Logo Blue (#1f64ae) for every action, and navy (#10224f) for headings and selected chips and tabs.
- **Do** make buttons, chips, tabs, bars and status labels full pills (999px).
- **Do** give cards a 1px hairline (#e3e6ec), 16 to 18px corners and the soft navy-tinted Card shadow; keep tiles inside a card flat.
- **Do** code subjects with emblem tints: maths green, sciences teal, computing orange, as tint background with deep foreground.
- **Do** grade performance green, orange, red-deep, and draw forecasts as dashed orange beside solid blue marks.
- **Do** ring at most one word per section heading with the blue hand-drawn ring.
- **Do** keep pictured objects in their artefact colours, Kalam handwriting and phone system type, and label every example as example data.
- **Do** honour reduced motion: drawings appear fully drawn and transitions switch off.

### Don't:
- **Don't** use Kalam, the phone system stack, examiner red or any artefact colour on interface chrome.
- **Don't** introduce a second interface typeface or a serif display face.
- **Don't** set uppercase tracked labels above headings; the only uppercase tracked text is the brand line printed on the pictured index card.
- **Don't** use hard or offset drop shadows for depth, or heavier shadows on hover.
- **Don't** show store logos before the app is live, or brand marks on the pictured devices.
- **Don't** show invented ratings, reviews, statistics, testimonials or press logos, and never copy the reference site's name, logo, images or copy.
- **Don't** apply this file to the staff and account pages until they are moved onto it deliberately.
