## Well-made education sites (quality bar)

- **Save My Exams:** one tight promise, a three-step strip (revise, test yourself, improve), subject chips, and named examiners as proof instead of stock photos.
- **Seneca:** student-voiced; an exam-board picker inside the first screen; real product screens with a student quote beside each.
- **BBC Bitesize:** entry points by age group, strong editorial rhythm, accessible.

## What this means for our home page

1. Everyone leads with "exam conditions, confidence, less anxiety" and a stock or hall photo. Show the real thing instead of saying it.
2. Nobody shows live availability. Seats left for each paper, on a visible timetable, would be unique to us.
3. Nobody shows what marking gives back. A sample marked script with marks for each question and a grade (personal details hidden) would set us apart once Part 3 marking exists.
4. Prices are usually hidden, "indicative" or in a PDF. Put "£35 per paper" on the first screen.
5. Booking is a Word or PDF form, email or phone almost everywhere. Pick a paper, pick a seat and pay by card in under a minute is the real difference.
6. Most sites speak to adult private candidates. Nobody speaks plainly to parents of 14 to 16 year olds. A clear parent and student split, as Save My Exams does, would help.
